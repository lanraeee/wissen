# Care and infrastructure thread: alignment with the CIO records

**Date:** 13 October 2026
**Branch:** `feat/care-and-infrastructure-thread`
**Status:** For trustee review. Nothing here amends the constitution.

## What changed on the site

The website now runs a thread across the three pillars: training young people for care and support, and for the infrastructure that makes communities work. The thread is training-led. It does not describe the charity delivering care or building infrastructure.

- Mission copy (home page) and the "Three Pillars" section (about story), both editable in Admin > Page Copy.
- Career Clarity Fair: booth categories "Care & Support" and "Infrastructure & Construction" added to the registration and booth dropdowns.
- Career pathways: two new routes, Care & Support and Infrastructure, with skills and a note that they are training routes, not clinical or certified engineering advice. Salary ranges are not published for these two until they are sourced.
- Career assessment: one new interest option, "Care & Community Support", scored to Social Impact Management. Appended at the end of the first (interests) question so existing answer codes do not move.
- Volunteer form: "Care & Support" and "Infrastructure & Community Build" roles, plus a note above the form on safeguarding for care and site-based roles.

## Mapping to the charitable objects (draft clause 2.1)

| Thread | Covered by the current objects? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Training in care and support skills (non-clinical) | Yes, under (a) advancement of education, through skills training | Must not be presented as clinical or regulated care. |
| Training in infrastructure and built-environment skills | Yes, under (a), as technical and vocational training | Training only. |
| Community projects run by trained young people | Yes, under (c) community development | Must be described as volunteer-led and local. |
| Direct care delivery (looking after people in need) | **No** | Would need a new or amended object, for example relief of those in need. |
| Building or maintaining infrastructure as the charity's own activity | **No** | Would need a new object and a delivery model. |
| Clinical care, or certified engineering practice | **No, and not intended** | The site says so explicitly. |

## Decisions for trustees

1. **Option A (recommended, current state):** keep the objects as drafted. Deliver the thread as training and community development. The website and form answers match the objects.
2. **Option B:** add objects for direct care or infrastructure delivery before the application is signed. This is a trustee decision and a constitution change. It needs re-checking against the Commission's guidance on charitable purposes before submission.

## SECTION 8: MEETINGS OF TRUSTEES

### 8.1 Calling Meetings (REQUIRED)

- [ ] Any charity trustee may call a meeting
- [ ] Trustees decide how meetings are called and what notice is required

### 8.2 Chairperson (REQUIRED)

- [ ] Trustees may appoint one of their number to chair meetings
- [ ] Chair appointment can be revoked at any time
- [ ] If no chair appointed, or chair unwilling to preside within 10 minutes, trustees present appoint one

### 8.3 Quorum (REQUIRED)

Select option:

**Standard quorum:**
- [ ] **2 trustees**, OR
- [ ] Number nearest to **1/3 of total trustees** (whichever is greater), OR
- [ ] Larger number as trustees decide
- [ ] Trustee with conflict of interest not counted in quorum

### 8.4 Decision-Making (REQUIRED)

**How are trustee decisions made:**

**Option A: Meetings only**
- [ ] All decisions made at properly convened meetings
- [ ] Majority vote decides questions

**Option B: Meetings + Written Resolutions**
- [ ] Decisions may be made:
  - At meetings, OR
  - By written resolution agreed by majority of all trustees
  - Written resolution must be signed/authenticated by majority of trustees
  - Trustees have **28 days** from circulation to respond
  - Resolution effective once majority signifies agreement

**Option C: Electronic Participation**
- [ ] Meetings may be held by electronic means
- [ ] Participants must be able to communicate with all others
- [ ] Must comply with rules for chairing and minutes

## SECTION 10: DECISIONS REQUIRING FULL MEMBERSHIP APPROVAL (REQUIRED)

These decisions must be made by all members (i.e., all trustees):

- [ ] Amending the constitution
- [ ] Amalgamating with other CIOs
- [ ] Transferring undertaking to another CIO
- [ ] Winding up or dissolving the CIO

**How are member decisions made:**

**Option A: General meetings only**
- [ ] Requires 14 days notice minimum
- [ ] Requires 75% majority to pass (for constitutional amendments/wind-up)

**Option B: Written resolutions**
- [ ] Members can vote by written resolution
- [ ] 75% majority required (for amendments/wind-up)
- [ ] Copy of resolution sent to all members
- [ ] Majority must signify agreement within **28 days**
- [ ] Delivered to principal office within 28 days

**Option C: General meetings + written resolutions**
- [ ] Either method permitted

## SECTION 11: GENERAL MEETINGS OF MEMBERS (REQUIRED)

### 11.1 Calling General Meetings

- [ ] Trustees may designate any trustees meeting as a general meeting of members
- [ ] Purpose: to discharge business required by law
- [ ] Any trustee may call such a meeting

### 11.2 Notice Requirements

- [ ] Minimum notice: **14 days** (can be shorter if majority agrees)
- [ ] Electronic notice acceptable (email) if properly addressed
- [ ] Hard copy notice for those not consenting to electronic

### 11.3 Procedures at Meetings

- [ ] Chairing, voting, and procedure same as trustee meetings (per Section 8)
- [ ] Quorum: 2 trustees or 1/3 of total, whichever greater
- [ ] Majority vote decides questions
- [ ] Chair has casting vote in tie

### 11.4 Optional Voting Methods (APPENDIX 1 - OPTIONAL)

- [ ] **Proxy Voting:** Members can appoint proxy to vote on their behalf
  - [ ] Proxy notice must be in writing
  - [ ] Must state member name, proxy person, and meeting reference
  - [ ] Signed by or on behalf of member
  - [ ] Delivered to CIO per constitution instructions

- [ ] **Postal/Email Voting:** Members can vote by post or email
  - [ ] Trustees decide if to allow
  - [ ] Minimum 21 days notice before voting deadline
  - [ ] Voting forms returned with member name and signature
  - [ ] Independent scrutineers count votes (minimum 2)
  - [ ] Results disclosed after in-person voting

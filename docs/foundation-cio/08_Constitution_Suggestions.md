# Constitution suggestions for trustee review

**Date:** 14 October 2026
**Status:** Suggestions only. Nothing here amends `03_Constitution_DRAFT.md`. The trustees decide which, if any, to adopt before the application is signed.

## Principles

1. **Every power serves the objects.** A new power is justified by how it advances education, poverty relief and economic independence, community development and social mobility, or research. None of these suggestions changes the objects.
2. **Objects stay as drafted.** Clause 15.2.1(a) bars changing the objects unless the new ones are equally charitable or more restrictive. Widening them is not on the table.
3. **Fix it before signing.** The Commission checks clause 2.1 word for word. Changes made now are simple. Changes after registration need the Commission's process.
4. **Check against current guidance.** The points below are drafting suggestions for trustees and a charity solicitor. They are not legal advice, and each needs checking against current Charity Commission guidance before adoption.

## 1. Investment (clauses 3.1(e) and 17.3.2(b))

- **Now:** trustees may invest funds not immediately required, "in such manner as the charity trustees shall determine" (3.1(e)), and in accordance with the Charities Act 2011 (17.3.2(b)).
- **Suggest:** an investment clause that states the statutory duties expressly (the Trustee Act 2000 standard investment criteria, and taking advice), plus a short reserves policy the trustees review each year.
- **Suggest:** an express power to make **programme-related investments**: investments made mainly to further the objects, not mainly for financial return, such as a loan or equity stake in a project that serves a named community. This is the most direct route to the "invest leftover money in projects" commitment now on the donate page.

## 2. Earned income (clause 3.2)

- **Now:** the CIO "shall not carry on any substantial trade or business other than in furtherance of its charitable objects." This limits income generation.
- **Suggest:** an express power to trade in furtherance of the objects, which covers ancillary trading (small, mission-linked income such as training fees or publications).
- **Suggest:** a power to set up a **wholly owned trading subsidiary** whose profits are gift-aided to the CIO. The subsidiary's trustees and controls need specific design, and the tax treatment needs advice before use.

## 3. Grant-making and beneficiaries (clause 17.3.2(c))

- **Now:** trustees may "make grants to other charities to further the objects." Grants to individuals and to non-charitable bodies are not mentioned.
- **Suggest:** an express power to make grants, bursaries and scholarships to **individuals** where this furthers the objects. The site already runs scholarships, so the constitution should cover them clearly.
- **Suggest:** a power to make grants and work with **overseas partners**, with due diligence, written agreements and monitoring. This matters for the Nigerian entity, Wissen Haus Empowerment Foundation NGA, and for any overseas delivery.

## 4. Connected companies and conflicts (clause 9)

- **Now:** clause 9 covers conflicts of interest in general terms.
- **Suggest:** a specific rule for **connected companies**, including Wissen Haus Ltd. It should require a written declaration, independent trustee approval, no preferential terms, and a register kept up to date. This should be settled before any collaboration with Wissen Haus Ltd, funded or otherwise.

## 5. Partnerships and structures

- **Suggest:** express powers to enter partnerships, joint ventures and consortia, to form or join companies (needed for the subsidiary in section 2), and to hold assets through nominees.
- **Check:** clause 3.1 already covers borrowing (3.1(a)), staff (3.1(d)) and fundraising (3.1(j)). Only add what is missing.

## 6. Trustee skills (clause 5.2)

- **Suggest:** a skills requirement for new trustees covering finance and investment, and Nigerian or overseas operations, so the board can oversee the powers above. Clause 5.2 currently sets the appointment process but no skills profile.

## 7. Changing the objects (Option B from doc 07)

- **Not recommended now.** Adding direct care, relief of those in need, or infrastructure delivery would require a constitution change, a re-check against the Commission's guidance, and a delivery model. Doc 07 sets this out. Revisit only when a specific funded project is planned.

## Order of work

1. Trustees decide on section 4 (connected companies) first, since it affects every collaboration.
2. Trustees decide on sections 1 to 3 together, since they change what the CIO can do with money.
3. A charity solicitor drafts the wording. Clause 2.1 stays unchanged.
4. Any change is reflected in `04_Charity_Commission_Form_Answers.md` before submission.
